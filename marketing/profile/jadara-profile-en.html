Who we are

One system for the owner and the accountant

Jadara Finance keeps the daily accounting work in one place. A sale or a purchase posts a balanced entry, and the same data prints in Arabic or English. It runs in the browser on a phone or a computer, and each company’s data stays separate.

Vision

A Saudi business runs its invoices, accounts, and financial statements from Jadara Finance, in one place built for VAT, zakat, and e-invoicing in the Kingdom.

Mission

We simplify the daily financial work. Sales, purchases, and inventory post their own entries. Reports are in Arabic and English. Each company’s data stays separate from the others.

Goals

Help the business issue invoices with a QR code, follow VAT and zakat, and produce a trial balance and financial statements, without a second program.

  • A tax invoice with a QR code. VAT is calculated and posted without a manual entry.
  • The general ledger, trial balance, and four financial statements come from the same journals.
  • Inventory, point of sale, and more than one branch when the business grows.
  • Activation within 24 hours of receiving the bank transfer.

Sections

Every section posts to the ledger

A sale, a purchase, or a payment updates the journals, inventory, and reports. The list below is the menu the company admin sees.

Daily work

  • DashboardColored tiles for sales, purchases, profit, cash, and customers, with a mix chart and columns.
  • SalesQuotations, sales orders, tax invoices, and returns.
  • PurchasesPurchase invoices. Requests, orders, and returns are on the Advanced plan.
  • Point of saleA till that reduces stock, posts the entry, and records the collection. Advanced plan.
  • TreasuryCash boxes, bank accounts, receipts, and payments.

Accounting

  • Chart of accountsA bilingual Saudi chart that can grow.
  • Journal entriesBalanced compound entries, with as many lines as needed.
  • Trial balanceOpening, movement, and closing balance.
  • Financial statementsIncome, financial position, cash flows, and changes in equity.
  • Fiscal periodsYears, opening balances, and a closing entry. A closed period accepts no new posting.
  • Data integrityConfirms the journals balance before the reports are relied on.

Basic covers accounting, invoices, and treasury. Advanced adds inventory, point of sale, branches, the VAT return, and zakat.

VAT, zakat, and e-invoicing
